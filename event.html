The Annual Involvement Fair

Student Life & Engagement | Friday, January 16, 6:00pm | Student Union Building

Welcome to your gateway to campus life. The Annual Involvement Fair is your one-stop opportunity to connect, explore, and find your community.

About This Event

The Annual Involvement Fair is your gateway to campus life, connecting students with hundreds of student organizations, campus departments, and local volunteer opportunities. Whether you want to discover a new hobby, explore a lifelong passion, or build your professional network, this event offers a central hub to find your community and make the most of your student experience.

What to Expect

This event will bring together hundreds of campus clubs, organizations, and departments so you can explore activities, meet student leaders and sign up.

Schedule

  • 6:00 PM - Doors open and check-in
  • 6:30 PM - Welcome and opening remarks
  • 7:00 PM - Main activity
  • 8:30 PM - Closing and networking

Accessibility and Participation

We are committed to providing an inclusive and accessible experience for all students. The fair features wheelchair accessible pathways, digital maps, and a dedicated sensory-friendly area at all times.

Event Info

  • Date: January 16, 2026
  • Time: 6:00 PM - 9:00 PM
  • Location: Student Union, Room 200
  • Organizer: Office of Student Engagement
  • Admission: Free for all students
